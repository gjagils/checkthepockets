# Quickly add to a savings goal

Under **Budgets › Savings** you'll find a list of savings plans. Next to each
plan is a number field and a **+ Add** button.

## One-off contribution

1. Open **Budgets › Savings**.
2. Enter the amount in the field for the right goal (e.g. `250`).
3. Click **+ Add** — the progress bar updates immediately.

The contribution is attached to the current month. You can also tie it to an
existing transaction (e.g. a transfer to your savings account) via the
`source_link` flow; that prevents double-counting.

## Monthly target

If you contribute a fixed amount every month, set it on the plan under
**Monthly contribution**. The app uses this to compute progress and the
remaining runway.

## Savings plans don't have a budget category

A savings plan lives separately from your budgets. Want to track the
"Savings" spending category too? Link the transactions to both the savings
plan and the category.
