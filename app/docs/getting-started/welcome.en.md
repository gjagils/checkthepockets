# Welcome to Check Your Pockets

Check Your Pockets is a personal finance app. You connect (or import) your bank
accounts, categorise transactions, build budgets and keep an eye on your portfolio
and mortgage — all in one place.

## What lives where?

- **Dashboard** — headline numbers for the current month.
- **Inbox** — new transactions that still need a category.
- **Transactions** — the full history with filters per account, month and category.
- **Budgets** — monthly limits per category.
- **Portfolio** — your investments and the spread across asset classes.

## First steps

1. Create at least one account under **Accounts**.
2. Import a CSV or link your bank under **Bank connection**.
3. Open the **Inbox** and categorise the first batch.

Good luck — and if anything is unclear, poke around this help section.
