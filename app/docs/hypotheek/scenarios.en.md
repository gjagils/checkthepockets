# Comparing mortgage scenarios

Under **Mortgage › Scenarios** you can run variants side-by-side: same house
with different rates, a different purchase price, a shorter term, and so on.

## New scenario

1. Go to **Mortgage › Scenarios**.
2. Click **+ Scenario**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Purchase price** and any **buyer costs**
   - **Down payment**
   - **Rate** and **fixed-rate period**
   - **Repayment type** (annuity, linear, interest-only)
   - **Term** in years
4. Save — the monthly payment and total cost show up immediately.

## Funda autofill

Have a Funda link? Paste it into the scenario form and the app pre-fills the
purchase price, property type and location details. Handy for crunching several
candidate homes quickly.

## Keeping rates up to date

Import rate sheets via **Mortgage › Rates** or enter them manually. New
scenarios default to the latest rate for the chosen repayment type and term.

## Household impact

Link a scenario to the household and you'll see a monthly impact straight
away: what's left after mortgage, fixed costs and insurance?
