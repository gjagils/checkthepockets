# Adding an asset to your portfolio

Under **Portfolio** you manage all your investments: ETFs, stocks, crypto,
bonds and cash. Every item has an asset type, a spread and (optionally) a
price source.

## New asset

1. Go to **Portfolio**.
2. Click **+ Asset**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Name** (e.g. "Vanguard FTSE All-World")
   - **Type** (ETF, stock, crypto, bond, cash)
   - **Current value** (amount invested or current valuation)
   - **Spread %** per asset class — must sum to 100%
4. Save.

## Connect a price source (optional)

For ETFs and stocks you can set a ticker + exchange. The app then fetches the
daily close via Yahoo-style providers and updates your portfolio value
automatically.

## Monthly contribution

Adding a fixed amount every month? Set it under **Monthly contribution** on
the asset — the dashboard forecast takes it into account.

## Spread overview

At `/portfolio/dashboard` you get an aggregated pie chart across all your
assets, based on the spread percentages you set per item.
