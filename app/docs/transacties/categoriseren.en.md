# Categorising transactions

New transactions land in your **Inbox** without a category. You can categorise
them in three ways — pick whichever fits the situation.

## 1. One at a time from the inbox

Open **Inbox** and pick a category per row from the dropdown. The transaction is
immediately marked as reviewed and leaves the inbox. Tip: use the account filter
to walk through one bank at a time.

## 2. Manually from the transactions list

On `/transactions` you can do the same, plus bulk actions via the checkboxes.
Handy when you want to recategorise a whole month after the fact.

## 3. Automatically via rules

Same counterparty showing up over and over (e.g. "Albert Heijn")? Go to
**Finance › Rules** and create a rule: when `counterparty` contains "Albert
Heijn", assign category **Groceries**. You can apply it to existing transactions
immediately; new imports get handled automatically.

## Miscategorised?

Open the transaction and pick another category in the dropdown. The change is
reflected instantly in budgets and dashboards.
